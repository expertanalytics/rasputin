# Where the land-cover clean-up step spends its time (master `3e6f87eb`)

A diagnosis for the design of the ROADMAP row "Speed up the land-cover
clean-up step" (increment 35). One `--stats` run and one cProfile run per
case, no optimisation, no code change. Not an acceptance run: no thread
sweep, no base-against-head comparison.

## Method

* Tree: the `cleanup-speed` worktree at master `3e6f87eb` (after increments 32
  and 34), its own `.venv` (Python 3.14.7, shapely 2.2.0 on GEOS 3.14.1,
  pyproj 3.8.0, `_core` built 2026-10-11 10:19 from this tree).
* Machine: Apple M1 Max, 10 cores, 32 GB. **Power: battery, 92 to 95 %,
  discharging** (`power-state.txt`, `pmset -g batt` before and after each
  run). Compare only with battery runs.
* Cases: the quick check's `numedalslagen` and `lagan` entries in
  `docs/benchmarks/quick/cases.toml`, the arguments exactly as there, plus
  `--out <scratch>.vtk --binary`; default threads (10). Lagan is CORINE in
  EPSG:3035 reprojected to EPSG:3006; Numedalslagen is CORINE already in the
  DEM's EPSG:25833.
* Runs, in this order, nothing else running:
  1. one warm-up run per case, then one run with `--stats <case>-stats.md`
     (the phase tables below; the stats run adds the quality pass, outside
     the phases);
  2. one cProfile run per case of the whole `rasputin mesh` command, without
     `--stats`: `prof_run.py` (here) wraps `tin_engine.cli.app` in
     `cProfile.Profile`; the raw profiles are `<case>.prof`, their pstats text
     `<case>-pstats.txt` (top 45 by cumulative, top 30 by own time, callees
     of every clean-up function), `<case>-pstats-detail.txt` and
     `lagan-pstats-take.txt` (the shapely and pyproj entries, the callees of
     the per-line clip in `_add`). Profiler overhead was small: 10.5 s wall
     against 9.6 s unprofiled on Numedalslagen, 20.9 s against 20.0 s on
     Lagan;
  3. one extra run on Numedalslagen only, `line_attrib.py`, which wraps the
     shapely calls and charges each to the `feature_input.py` line that made
     it (`numedalslagen-by-line.txt`). It is there for one purpose: to split
     the two clips inside `_clean`, which cProfile cannot (both are list
     comprehensions inlined into `_clean` and both call
     `shapely.intersection`). Its absolute times are not a measurement: the
     wrapping slowed that run to 26.5 s and even the single `coverage_clean`
     call took 3.4 s against 1.22 s under cProfile, which is not explained.
     Only its ratio between line 420 and line 444 is used below. The same
     run on Lagan was killed by the 600 s watchdog and not repeated.

Meshes went to the session scratchpad and are regenerated by the commands
in the two `<case>-stats.md` files (their first line).

## 3. The largest sub-steps

1. **The outline rule, `snap_to_outline` (feature_input.py:640): 2.80 s and
   2.50 s, 41 % and 32 % of the step.** Most of it is GEOS, not the Python
   loop: `union_all` of the rebuilt parts and of the loops (lines 680, 684,
   687) plus `make_valid` and `is_valid` of the rebuilt shells (lines 676,
   683) and `_loops`'s `make_valid` (line 710) are 1.39 s on Numedalslagen
   and 1.69 s on Lagan. The Python loop over ring vertices, `_Outline.ring`
   (line 776), is 0.86 s and 0.61 s, of which only 0.25 s and 0.13 s is its
   own Python time (the rest is one `STRtree.query` per ring and
   `query_nearest` in `_place`); `_chains` (line 737) adds 0.22 s and 0.11 s
   of Python.
2. **The two clips, `shapely.intersection` at lines 420 and 444: 1.43 s and
   1.21 s, 21 % and 16 % of the step.** All GEOS. On Numedalslagen the clip
   of the 25 merged class polygons to the domain (line 444) is the larger
   of the two, about 0.85 s against about 0.58 s for the 7 932 per-feature
   clips to the read region (ratio from the by-line run); Lagan's split was
   not measured. On Lagan a third clip, `_add`'s per-line `intersection` and
   `covers` against the domain (lines 395 and 402), costs another 1.46 s
   (19 % of the step); `covers` is 179 µs per call there against 2 µs on
   Numedalslagen, which is not explained by this profile.
3. **The repair, `shapely.coverage_clean` (line 427): 1.22 s and 0.86 s,
   18 % and 11 % of the step.** One GEOS call over all the clipped
   polygons; no Python in it.

Outside the step but inside the `features clip` phase on Lagan: the
reprojection of every CORINE polygon from EPSG:3035 to EPSG:3006 before the
clean-up, 4.08 s (20 % of the run), all in pyproj's `Transformer.transform`
(`crs.py:48`, called once per polygon with that polygon's vertices).
Numedalslagen has no reprojection.

The simplifier (increment 32, C++) is 0.69 s and 0.71 s; `_outline_lines`
(increment 32's cut where a line lies on the outline) is 0.30 s and 0.74 s,
on Lagan a quarter of it building a new `STRtree` of the outline's segments
for each of the 24 polygons (line 722).

## Files

* `<case>-stats.md`: the `--stats` report of the stats run (section 1).
* `<case>.prof`: the raw cProfile data; `<case>-pstats*.txt`: its text
  dumps, from `prof_dump.py`, `prof_detail.py` and `prof_more.py` (described in
  the method).
* `numedalslagen-by-line.txt`: the by-line attribution run (ratios only).
* `prof_run.py`, `line_attrib.py`, `prof_dump.py`: the scripts that produced
  them, run as `.venv/bin/python <script> <output> mesh <args...>` from the
  tree.
* `power-state.txt`: `pmset -g batt` before and after each run.
