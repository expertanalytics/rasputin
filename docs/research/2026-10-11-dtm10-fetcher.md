# Can `rasputin fetch` get Kartverket's DTM10?

Status: research note, 2026-10-11, `@architect`, for Ola's idea "a DTM
fetcher, I don't know if is possible?" (2026-10-11). Research only: no design
and no code. Every figure below was measured on 2026-10-11 with `curl` under a
generic user agent and no credential; what was not measured is marked so.

**Verdict: possible, without a login, on two plain-HTTP routes; the per-tile
route is the one a fetcher should take, and it carries a data caveat.**

## 1. Terms

- The Geonorge record of "DTM 10 Terrengmodell (UTM33)" (uuid
  `dddbb667-1303-4ac5-8640-7ec04c0e3918`, read as JSON from
  `https://kartkatalog.geonorge.no/api/getdata/<uuid>`) says `AccessConstraints`
  "Åpne data", licence "Creative Commons BY 4.0 (CC BY 4.0)" with the link
  `https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/`, owner Kartverket.
- Kartverket's terms of use (`kartverket.no/api-og-data/vilkar-for-bruk`):
  open data under CC BY 4.0, credit "© Kartverket", the name shown wherever
  the product or an extract of it is used.
- **Caveat:** two sibling records say **CC BY-NC 4.0**: "DTM 10 Terrengmodell
  (UTM32)" (`fd851873-...`) and the yearly snapshot "DTM 10 Terrengmodell
  (UTM33) 2019" (`4e82d852-...`). The "Historiske versjoner" series
  (`342be245-...`) says CC BY 4.0. A fetcher must read the licence from the
  record of the dataset it fetches, not assume Kartverket's default; UTM33's
  current record is CC BY 4.0, which is what `NOTICE.md` already credits.
- hoydedata.no's own Geonorge record (`91bd03b1-...`) says "Åpne data" and
  names no licence; Kartverket's terms page is what makes its exports CC BY
  4.0. **Not read on hoydedata.no itself.**

## 2. Route A: Geonorge, one zip per 50 km tile (the 2013-based model)

Measured:

- Capabilities, `GET https://nedlasting.geonorge.no/api/capabilities/<uuid>`:
  area and format selection supported, no polygon clipping,
  `deliveryNotificationByEmail: false`.
- Code lists: one projection (EPSG:25833), two formats ("TIFF", "DEM "), 254
  areas of type "ikke spesifisert" named `6400-1` to `7910-3`: the national
  50 km cells, `RRCC-q` with `RR` the 100 km row (northing / 100 km), `CC`
  the 100 km column, and `q` the quadrant (1 NE, 2 SE, 3 SW, 4 NW; checked
  against the tie points of tiles 6400_1 to 6400_4 and 7908_3). So a bbox
  maps to tile names with no footprint index.
- Order, `POST https://nedlasting.geonorge.no/api/order` with `"email": ""`
  and one order line (uuid, area `6400-1`, projection 25833, format TIFF):
  HTTP 200, `status: "ReadyForDownload"`, a `downloadUrl` under
  `/api/download/order/<ref>/<fileId>`. No login, no email.
- The same files have **stable direct URLs, no order needed**:
  `https://nedlasting.geonorge.no/geonorge/Basisdata/DTM10UTM33/TIFF/Basisdata_<cell>_Celle_25833_DTM10UTM33_TIFF.zip`,
  listed in the Atom feed
  `https://nedlasting.geonorge.no/geonorge/ATOM-feeds/DTM10UTM33_AtomFeedTIFF.xml`
  (254 entries, each with the link, cell name and `updated` 2018-01-26).
- Size: `HEAD` on all 254 zips: **7.51 GB in all**, mean 29.6 MB, max 56.1 MB,
  min 8 kB (sea cells). Tile 6400-1 (40.2 MB zip, 4 s) unzips to one file,
  `6400_1_10m_z33.tif`: 5041 × 5041 float32, LZW, tiled, EPSG:25833, NoData
  −32767, pixel corner at (49800, 6500200). The zip holds nothing else.
- **The data is the 2013 model**: the record's abstract says the files were
  last updated in 2013, maintenance is not prioritised, and the current DTM10
  is on hoydedata.no.
- **Lattice:** hoydedata.no's tile 6400_1 has 5051 × 5051 cells with its
  corner at (49745, 6500255): the two sets are offset by half a cell. The
  mosaic (`tin_engine.mosaic`, increment 15a) refuses tiles off one lattice,
  so a cache must keep the two apart.

## 3. Route B: hoydedata.no, the current model, one zip for the country

Measured:

- The web application's list of nationwide exports is
  `GET https://hoydedata.no/laserservices/rest/GetFileDownloads.ashx`: JSON,
  115 entries over UTM zones 32, 33 and 35; for zone 33 `produktnavn` "DTM10",
  `zipnavn` `DTM10_UTM33_20261009.zip`, 18 670.8 MB, `las_landsdekkende_id`
  54 (also DTM50 at 781 MB and DOM10).
- Download: `GET https://hoydedata.no/laserservices/rest/DownloadFile.ashx?id=54`
  answers 200 with `Content-Length: 19577752900` and
  `Content-Disposition: filename=DTM10_UTM33_20261009.zip`, no cookie, no
  login. A `Range: bytes=0-63` request was answered with the **whole body**
  (200, not 206), so a remote reader cannot seek into the zip; it has to be
  downloaded in full (about 8 MB/s was seen: 488 MB in 60 s, so roughly 40
  minutes to an hour).
- Ola's copy of the previous export (`../rasputin_data/DTM10_UTM33_20260925/`)
  is 254 `.tif` files, 20.41 GB, mean 80 MB, max 144 MB, each with a `.tfw`
  and a `.aux.xml`, 5051 × 5051 float32 LZW, as the committed tile
  `tests/fixtures/dem_archive/7908_3_10m_z33.tif`.
- The application also has `StartExport.ashx` for clipped exports; in the
  bundle it is called with the user's login state, and the help pages
  describe export orders delivered by email. **Not tried**; treated as needing
  an account.

## 4. What a fetcher would look like, and what it costs

A new catalogue entry `dtm10` in `src_python/tin_engine/sources.py`, of a new
kind `zip-tiles` beside `one-cog` and `cog-tiles`: the Atom feed as
`tile_list_url`, the direct zip URL as `url_template` with the cell name, and
the cell name computed from the requested box as in section 2, so `fetch`'s
plan step (`fetch/plan.py`) needs no footprint file. The download unzips the
one member into `<cache>/dtm10/<cell>.tif`; the mosaic then reads the cache
directory as it reads any `--dem DIR`. `NOTICE.txt` from the record's licence
text and "© Kartverket". Python only; the C++ core is untouched (CLAUDE.md
§2's I/O boundary), and no new dependency (`zipfile` and the existing HTTP
client). Rough size: 80 to 150 production lines plus tests, like 23a-2.

Costs and limits a design must state:

- Route A fetches 2013 data. A catchment-sized domain costs a few tiles (30 to
  56 MB each); the whole country 7.5 GB.
- Route B is the current model but 19.6 GB per fetch, unseekable, and the
  export is replaced every few weeks (2026-09-25, 2026-10-09 seen), so a cache
  key needs the file's date. It fits a `rasputin fetch dtm10-current` that
  downloads once and unpacks into the cache, not a per-domain fetch.
- Mixing the two in one `--dem DIR` is refused by the mosaic (half-cell
  offset); the cache keeps them under different source names.
- The Geonorge tiles' CC BY 4.0 must be read from the UTM33 record at design
  time and recorded in the source's `licence_note`, given the BY-NC siblings.

## 5. What was not verified

- Nothing on hoydedata.no's own pages states the licence; section 1 infers it
  from Kartverket's terms.
- Only tile 6400-1 was downloaded and read from route A; the other 253 were
  only `HEAD`ed.
- Route B's zip was not downloaded past 488 MB; its member list is assumed to
  match Ola's unpacked copy of the previous export.
- Whether Geonorge or hoydedata.no rate-limits or blocks a scripted client
  with hundreds of requests: 254 `HEAD`s in one burst passed.
