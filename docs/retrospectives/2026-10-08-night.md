## 2. Lead time (Ola's ask, the baseline)

**How it is computed.** `python3 docs/retrospectives/lead_time.py <since>
[branch ...]` reads git only. Per PR merged into master: *start* is the first
commit on its branch, *build* is start to the branch's last commit (design,
tests, code, review, records), *wait* is the last commit to the merge (Ola's
yes, the push, CI, the queue). An unmerged branch's wait runs to now. This
matches DORA's "change lead time", "the amount of time it takes for a change
to go from committed to version control to deployed in production"
(dora.dev/guides/dora-metrics, read 2026-10-08), with the merge as the
deployment. It misses work done before the first commit: 30d's first
`@perf` run started at 08:46 on 2026-10-07 and was lost to sleep; its branch
starts at 22:21.

   the gates are green. Today a spoken grant is not enough at night: the push

   record the named branches, and `guard_push.py` would let one push and one

   lines with tests in governed files, by day. *Default:* yes, build it.
   This is Ola's approval boundary; "no" keeps today's rule.

   two checks: every added step timed on both catchments with default flags,

   2 h 23 min of rework, for about 15 minutes of review. *Cost:* the design
   review rule Ola approved on 2026-10-07, plus the two lines. *Default:* yes.

   folder per agent, and the lessons file moved out of the shared root), P5
   and P12. *Saves:* a truncated recap after a compaction costs a guessed
   round (hours); tonight's lost lessons file. *Cost:* about 60 lines, one
   writer for half a day. *Default:* yes.

   merged PRs' build and wait hours (from `lead_time.py`), and each
   `@orchestrator` check compares them with section 2's table. *Saves:* finds
   a regression like 10-06's within a day. *Cost:* about 15 lines, governed.
   *Default:* yes.

   and `merge_master.py` falls back to the main checkout's venv when a
   worktree has none. *Saves:* the manual reruns from the main checkout, and
   a defect that only the real data shows reaching review. *Cost:* about 5
   lines in `tests/`, about 10 in `tools/merge_master.py`. *Default:* yes.
