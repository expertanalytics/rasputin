## 3. Proposals, with evidence

1. **A reviewer handback without a readable `Review:` line is sent back.**
   In `record_review.py`, on `PreToolUse` of a reviewer's
   `SubagentHandback` only, deny when no line parses, with the reason
   showing the exact form `Review: APPROVED WITH WORDINGS <40-hex sha>`;
   the reviewer is still running and rewrites it. Evidence: lesson 1, two
   granted pushes refused, four unrecorded verdicts tonight. Cost: about
   10 lines and three tests in a governed hook; it changes h20 §3.3's
   "never blocks" for this one case. Cheaper half, no code: `reviewer.md`
   step 1 shows the line in full.
2. **Pre-push review does not wait on CI.** One sentence in `reviewer.md`'s
   CI precondition: before the first push there is no CI; the verdict is
   on the head as reviewed, and CI is read at merge (`CLAUDE.md` §4).
   Owner `@architect`. Evidence: lesson 1's 77 and 78 lines.
3. **When the window opens, the main session hears it.** h26's Stop hook
   already reads the away flag; a `UserPromptSubmit` or `SessionStart`
   note "away mode is on until …; grants: …" would have saved 1.3's three
   hours. Cost: about 10 lines and a settings entry (Ola's edit).
