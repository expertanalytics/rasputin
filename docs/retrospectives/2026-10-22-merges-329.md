## 3. Is #327's rule clear enough?

Yes. `.claude/REQUIRED-READING.md` says "`@reviewer` runs once on any branch
before its first push, whether or not the branch contains production code",
and `docs/increments/README.md` lists "`@reviewer` before every push" among

missing is a check at the moment of the push: the away grant reads
`reviews.jsonl`, an attended push does not. Proposal 2.

## 5. Proposals

1. **Keep the main checkout current.** `tools/duties.py` gains a duty
   "fast-forward the main checkout (N commits behind; hooks or rules
   changed)" when `HEAD..origin/master` touches `.claude/`, `CLAUDE.md` or
   `tools/`, so it shows in the recap, on every prompt and in Ola's status
   line. The main session then runs `git pull --ff-only origin master` in
   the main checkout, a ref write that needs Ola's yes. Evidence: finding 2.
   Cost: about 12 lines in a governed tool, by day; no rule words.
2. **The push guard shows the review.** When `guard_push.py` asks before an
   attended push, pull request or merge, its question adds "no approving
   review recorded for <sha>" when `reviews.jsonl` holds none, reusing
   `push_grant.review_of`. Evidence: finding 4, and section 3 of the night check (PR #330).
   Cost: about 10 lines, governed, by day; no rule words.
3. **A refused pull request may be redone in the form its reason names.**
   `NOT_BARE` names the single-quoted `--title`, and the redo sentence of
   *Unattended mode* reads "a refused amend or pull request, only in the
   form its reason names". Evidence: §1, 29 h 31 min across three PRs.
   Supersedes Q-A of `2026-10-21-merge-lsm38.md`. Cost: about 4 rule words
   and 2 lines in `tools/push_grant.py`.
