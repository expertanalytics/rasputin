## 5. Proposals (Ola decides)

- **A. The serial leg's cost.** Options: (a) keep it, about 18 min per code
  PR; (b) run the serial leg only in the merge queue run, which still gates
  every merge, and keep PR runs parallel (about 9 min per round of
  findings; the queue stays about 18); (c) record that Ola's yes to the
  parallel runner overrides §X and drop the serial leg. Default: (b). Cost:
  one condition in `.github/workflows/main.yaml`, one layout test, a status
  line in `h15-ci-speed.md` §5 X. No rule words.
- **B. First use of a merged tool.** The post-merge check of a tool runs the
  tool's own read command (for the pipes, `work_pipes.py next`) and flags an
  empty result while worktrees are ahead of master; and `work_pipes.py`
  itself prints "no items loaded; N branches ahead of master" instead of
  idle. Cost: about 10 lines and a test in `work_pipes.py`; no rule words.
- **C. `set … waiting` refused.** Only `ask` enters `waiting`, so every
  waiting item carries a question and a default. Cost: about 3 lines and a
  test.
- **D. Note files deleted by a hook, not by memory.** Four reminders have
  not worked. `.claude/hooks/record_review.py`, which already runs on
  every `SubagentHandback` (`.claude/settings.json`), deletes the note file named in that agent's brief when its
  handback arrives. One-off now: the main session deletes the 118 after the
  recap has printed them (the rule allows it for lost spawners). Cost: about
  15 lines in the hook, with a test; the sentence "The spawner deletes it on
  reading the handback — never the subagent" can then go (cut about 10
  words).
- **E. Drop the `QUEUE:` line; the pipes are the queue.** One-off: each of
  the 25 `QUEUE:` lines either becomes a `work_pipes.py add` or is dropped as
  done; rulings and `STATE` lines go to their increment files or here; stale
  `ASK OLA` lines go to Ola once, numbered, then out. Rule: in
  `REQUIRED-READING.md`, cut "`QUEUE:` (at most one) or" and replace "the
  `QUEUE:` line names at least one fallback that needs no ruling and writes no
  governed path, and one such item stays queued" with "the pipes hold at
  least one `--fallback` item, and one stays queued" (`work_pipes.py` already
  refuses `--fallback` with `--governed`). Net **-15 words**; the `QUEUE`
  count in `session_state.py` goes too.
- **F. Duties by name.** A check commit retires the merges whose `#N` its
  subject names; a subject with none retires by time, as now. Cost: about 10
  lines and tests in `duties.py`.
- **G. Records for CI changes.** A CI-only change still updates its design
  file's status and `## Review` (deviations 1 and 2), and a layout test pins
  the rule it restores (deviation 3).

## 6. Lessons

- A proposal that changes CI states its effect on the whole run's wall time,
  not one job's; the slowest job sets the run.
- A question to Ola that reverses a gain he was just told about names the
  number in the same line.
- A rule enforced only by a warning in the recap is not enforced: the note
  files and `session.md` were warned about four and more times.
